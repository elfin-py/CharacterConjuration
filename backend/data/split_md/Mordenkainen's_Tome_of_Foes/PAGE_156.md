REGIONAL EFFECTS 
The region containing Yeenoghu's lair is warped by his 
magic, creating one or more of the following effects: 
• Within 1 mile of the lair, large iron spikes grow out of 
the ground and stone surfaces. Yeenoghu impales the 
bodies of the slain on these spikes. 
• Predatory beasts within 6 miles of the lair become un­
usually savage, killing far more than what they need 
for food. Carcasses of prey are left to rot in an unnatu­
ral display of wasteful slaughter. 
• If a humanoid spends at least 1 hour within 1 mile 
of the lair, that creature must succeed on a DC 17 
Wisdom saving throw or descend into a madness 
determined by the Madness of Yeenoghu table. A 
creature that succeeds on this saving throw can't be 
affected by this regional effect again for 24 hours.
