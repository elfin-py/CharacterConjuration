REACTIONS 
Fearful Voice (Recharge 5-6). In response to taking damage, 
Hutijin utters a dreadful word of power. Each creature within 30 
feet of him that isn't a devil must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom 
saving throw or become frightened of him for 1 minute. A 
creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its 
turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that 
saves against this effect is immune to Hutijin's Fearful Voice 
for 24 hours.
