# Earth 'Tremor

1st-level evocation 
Casting Time: 1 action 
Range: 10 feet 
Components: V, S 
Duration: Instantaneous 
You cause a tremor in the ground within range. Each 
creature other than you in that a rea must make a Dex-
terity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 
ld6 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. If the 
ground in that area is loose earth or stone, it becomes 
difficult terrain until cleared, with each 5-foot-diameter 
portion requiring at least 1 minute to clear by hand.
