# Complications

The description of each activity includes a discussion 
of complications you can throw at the characters. The 
consequences of a complication might spawn entire 
adventures, introduce NPCs to vex the party, or give the 
characters headaches or advantages in any number of 
other ways.

Each of these sections has a table that offers possible 
complications. You can roll to determine a complication

own, and then share it with the player.
