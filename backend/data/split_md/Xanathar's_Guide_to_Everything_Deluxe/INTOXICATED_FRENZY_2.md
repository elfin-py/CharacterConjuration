# Intoxicated Frenzy

At 17th level, you gain the ability to make an overwhelm-
ing number of attacks against a group of enemies. Whe n 
you use your Flurry of Blows, you can make up to three 
additional attacks with it (up to a total of five Flurry of 
Blows attacks), provided that each Flurry of Blows at-
tack targets a different creature this turn.
