# Wand Of Pyrotechnics

Wand, comm on 
This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use 
an action to expend 1 of its charges and create a harm-
less burst of multicolored light at a point you can see 
up to 60 feet away. The burst of light is accompanied 
by a crackJing noise that can be heard up to 300 feet 
away. The light is as bright as a torch flame but lasts 
only a second. 
The wand regains l d6 + 1 expended charges daily at 
dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. 
On a 1, the wand erupts in a harmless pyrotechnic dis-
play and is destroyed.
