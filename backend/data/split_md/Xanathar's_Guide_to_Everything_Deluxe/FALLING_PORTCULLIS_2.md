# Falling Portcullis

Simple trap (Jevel 1- 4, moderate threat) 
Some folk who build dungeons, such as mad wizards 
in search of new victims, have no intention of allowing 
their visitors to make an easy escape. A falling portcullis 
trap can be especially devious ii it causes a portcullis 
to drop some distance away from the pressure plate 
that activates the trap. Although the trap is deep in the 
dungeon, the portcullis closes off the dungeon entrance, 
which is hundreds of feet away, meaning that adventur-
ers don't know they are trapped until they decide to head 
for the exit. 
Trigger. A creature that steps on the pressure plate 
triggers the trap. 
Effect. An iron portcullis drops from the ceiling, 
blocking an exit or a passageway. 
Countermeasures. A successful DC 20 Wisdom (Per-
ception) check reveals the pressure plate. A successful 
DC 20 Dexterity check using thieves' tools disables it, 
and a check with a total of 5 or lower triggers the trap.
