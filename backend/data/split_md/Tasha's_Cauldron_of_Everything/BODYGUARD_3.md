# Bodyguard

----
Fighting Style: Interception,* Protection 
Maneuvers: Bait and Switch,* Disarming Attack, 
Goading Attack, Grappling Strike* 
Feats: Alert, Observant, Sentinel, Tough 
Love, money, or some other obligation motivates you 
to place your own body between harm and the one 
you're sworn to protect. You have honed the ability 
to sniff out potential threats and see your charge 
through dangerous situations.
