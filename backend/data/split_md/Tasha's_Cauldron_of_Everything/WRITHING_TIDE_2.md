# Writhing Tide

7th-level Swarmkeeper feature 
You can condense part of your swarm into a focused 
mass that lifts you up. As a bonus action, you gain 
a flying speed of 10 feet and can hover. This effect 
lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all ex­
pended uses when you finish a long rest.
