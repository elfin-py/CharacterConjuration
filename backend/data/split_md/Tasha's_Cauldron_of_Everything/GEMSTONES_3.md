# Gemstones

An inventory of the room reveals the following gem­
stones scattered across the floor: eighteen pieces of 
jade, sixteen onyxes, fourteen amethysts, thirteen 
sapphires, twelve rubies, nine pieces of amber, eight 
citrines, five garnets, and one piece of quartz. 
If a creature attempts to leave the room with any 
of these stones, the gem disappears and an angry 
spirit trapped inside it is released. The spirit man­
ifests as a hostile undead creature of your choice, 
such as a ghost, specter, or skeleton. When this 
creature is reduced to 0 hit points, its form dissi­
pates, leaving no trace of itself behind.
