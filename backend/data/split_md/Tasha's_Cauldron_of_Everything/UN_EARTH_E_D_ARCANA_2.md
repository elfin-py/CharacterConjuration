# Un Earth E D  Arcana

Much of the material in this book originally appeared 
in Unearthed Arcana, a series of online articles we 
publish to explore rules that might officially become 
part of the game. Some Unearthed Arcana offerings 
don't end up resonating with fans and are set aside. 
The Unearthed Arcana material that inspired the op­
tions in the following chapters was well received and, 
thanks to feedback from thousands of D&D fans, has 
been refined into the official forms presented here.
