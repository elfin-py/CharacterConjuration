# It'S All Optional

Everything in this book is optional. Each group, 
guided by the DM, decides which of these options, 
if any, to incorporate into a campaign. You can use 
some, all, or none of them. We encourage you to 
choose the ones that fit best with your campaign's 
story and with your_ group's style of play. 
Whatever options you choose to use, this book re­
lies on the rules in the Player's Handbook, Monster 
Manual, and Dungeon Master's Guide, and it can be 
paired with the options in Xanathar's Guide to Ev­
erything and other D&D books.
