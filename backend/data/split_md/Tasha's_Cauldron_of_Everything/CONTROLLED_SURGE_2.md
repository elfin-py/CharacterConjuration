# Controlled Surge

14th-level Path of Wild Magic feature 
Whenever you roll on the Wild Magic table, you 
can roll the die twice and choose which of the two 
effects to unleash. If you roll the same number on 
both dice, you can ignore the number and choose 
any effect on the table.
