# Infectious Fury

10th-level Path of the Beast feature 
When you hit a creature with your natural weapons 
while you are raging, the beast within you can curse 
your target with rabid fury. The target must succeed 
on a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your 
Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus) or 
suffer one of the following effects (your choice): 
• The target must use its reaction to make a melee 
attack against another creature of your choice that 
you can see. 
• The target takes 2d12 psychic damage. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all ex­
pended uses when you finish a long rest.
