# Raising The Difficulty

You can increase this puzzle's difficulty by changing 
the word search's letters to use another alphabet, 
such as those presented in the Player's Handbook. 
Alternatively, you can create an entirely new code to 
replace the letters, requiring the characters to find a 
cipher to reveal the tiles' meanings before they can 
undertake the puzzle and cross the room safely. 
You can also increase the difficulty by introducing 
trap variants, as described below.
