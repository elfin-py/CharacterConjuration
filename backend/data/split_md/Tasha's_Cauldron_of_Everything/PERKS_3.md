# Perks

A group patron offers your party a number of perks 
for your service. These range from standard busi­
ness arrangements, such as a steady wage and ac­
cess to staff facilities, to extraordinary boons, such 
as audiences with powerful figures or exceptions 
from certain laws. Specific perks are presented in 
the description of each group patron. 
The DM should not feel limited to providing only 
the perks noted in each group patron's description. 
Patrons give a party access to solutions and support 
they wouldn't have otherwise, and a patron can use 
their varied resources to guide their agents or pre­
pare them for greater adventures.
