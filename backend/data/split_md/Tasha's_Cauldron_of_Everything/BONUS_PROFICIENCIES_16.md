# Bonus Proficiencies

1st-level Expert feature 
The sidekick gains proficiency in one saving throw 
of your choice: Dexterity, Intelligence, or Charisma. 
In addition, the sidekick gains proficiency in five 
skills of your choice, and it gains proficiency with 
light armor. If it is a humanoid or has a simple or 
martial weapon in its stat block, it also gains profi­
ciency with all simple weapons and with two tools of 
your choice.
