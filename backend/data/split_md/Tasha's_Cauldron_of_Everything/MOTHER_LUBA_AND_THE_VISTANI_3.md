# Mother Luba And The Vistani

The creator of the Tarokka of Souls, Mother Luba was 
one of the most influential leaders of the Vistani. For 
untold generations, the Vistani have wandered the 
Shadowfell, which includes terrifying demiplanes like 
the vampire-haunted realm of Barovia. These travelers 
have learned many secrets of these domains and en­
countered countless others wandering amid the Shad­
owfell's horrors. Most Vistani bands accept well-inten­
tioned wayfarers from diverse walks and of disparate 
origins, embracing any who seek to find a home amid 
the endless roads and vistas hidden amid the mists. 
A halfling Vistani, Mother Luba led one of the largest 
groups ofVistani in the Shadowfell. She hailed from 
the same world that Count Strahd von Zarovich and 
Madam Eva came from, and she created a community 
of kindness and resilience-ever rare to find in the 
Plane of Shadow. She led her people in welcoming 
strangers, feeding the hungry, and defying the cruel. 
She and Madam Eva were once friends, until Madam

"We may wander amid the shadows," Mother Luba 
said. "But we must ever serve as a light to our fellow 
travelers."

mists, leaving behind only the Tarokka of Souls. It is
