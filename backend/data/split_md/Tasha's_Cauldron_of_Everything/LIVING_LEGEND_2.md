# Living Legend

20th-level Oath of Glory feature 
You can empower yourself with the legends­
whether true or exaggerated-of your great deeds. 
As a bonus action, you gain the following benefits 
for 1 minute: 
• You are blessed with an otherworldly presence, 
gaining advantage on all Charisma checks. 
• Once on each of your turns when you make a 
weapon attack and miss, you can cause that attack 
to hit instead. 
If you fail a saving throw, you can use your reac­
tion to reroll it. You must use this new roll. 
Once you use this bonus action, you can't use it 
again until you finish a long rest, unless you expend 
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
