# Lancer

Fighting Style: Dueling 
Maneuvers: Lunging Attack, Menacing Attack, Pre­
cision Attack, Pushing Attack 
Feats: Heavy Armor Master, Mounted Combatant, 
Savage Attacker 
When the cavalry is called in, that means you. You 
ride out to greet your enemy with the point of your 
weapon. As you charge, the ground trembles, and 
only the heaviest blows can deter you.
