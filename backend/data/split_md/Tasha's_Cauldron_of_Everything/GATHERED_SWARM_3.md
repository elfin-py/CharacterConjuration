# Gathered Swarm

3rd-level Swarmkeeper feature 
A swarm of intangible nature spirits has bonded 
itself to you and can assist you in battle. Until you 
die, the swarm remains in your space, crawling on 
you or flying and skittering around you within your 
space. You determine its appearance, or you gener­
ate its appearance by rolling on the Swarm Appear­
ance table.
