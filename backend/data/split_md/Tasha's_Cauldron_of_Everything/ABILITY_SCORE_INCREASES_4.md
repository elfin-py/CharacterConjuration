# Ability Score Increases

Whenever the sidekick gains the Ability Score Im­
provement feature, adjust anything in its stat block 
that relies on an ability modifier that you increase. 
For example, if the sidekick has an attack that uses 
its Strength modifier, increase the attack's mod­
ifiers to hit and damage if the Strength modifier 
increases. 
If it's unclear whether a melee attack in the stat 
block uses Strength or Dexterity, the attack can 
use either.
