# Academy Contact

How much autonomy you have in choosing your 
missions and how often you're expected to perform 
on the academy's behalf depends largely on your 
place in the institution's hierarchy. As students, new 
professors, or support staff, you aid the work of a 
senior professor or entire department. If you are fur­
ther along in your career, you have your own goals 
and assistants, but you still take on assignments to 
further the goals of esteemed experts, deans, or the 
academy as a whole. In any of these cases, a specific 
contact manages your relationship to the academy. 
Roll or pick from the Academy Contact table to de­
termine who manages the relationship between you 
and the academy.
