# Duelist

Fighting Style: Dueling, Two-Weapon Fighting 
Maneuvers: Evasive Footwork, Feinting Attack, 
Lunging Attack, Parry, Precision Attack, Riposte 
Feats: Defensive Duelist, Dual Wielder, Observant, 
Savage Attacker, Weapon Master 
You regard the duel as a proud tradition, a test of 
skill and wits that brings honor to those who can de­
feat an enemy while respecting the art. Your search 
for improvement is a consuming passion, and you 
draw on the expertise of the masters who've come 
before you as you work to perfect your form.
