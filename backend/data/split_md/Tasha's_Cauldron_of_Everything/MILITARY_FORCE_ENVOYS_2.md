# Military Force Envoys

You might join a military force for a wide range of 
reasons, or the military has reasons for seeking 
you out. Those with specialized skills contribute to 
a range of missions, from infiltration and mystical 
operations to diplomacy and strategy. Perhaps your 
past deeds or the will of your family pushes you 
toward military service, regardless of whether you 
believe you're suited to such a life. 
The Military Force Envoy Roles table suggests a 
variety of military roles you could fill and the back­
grounds frequently associated with each role.
