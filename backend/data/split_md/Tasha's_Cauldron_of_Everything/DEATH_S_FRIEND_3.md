# Death 'S Friend

17th-level Phantom feature 
Your association with death has become so close 
that you gain the following benefits: 
• When you use your Wails from the Grave, you can 
deal the necrotic damage to both the first and the 
second creature. 
• At the end of a long rest, a soul trinket appears in 
your hand if you don't have any soul trinkets, as 
the spirits of the dead are drawn to you.
