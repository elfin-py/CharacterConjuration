The world's mysteries are innumerable, but you pur­
sue them with vigor. As operatives for an academy, 
you seek to unravel the secrets of existence and the

discover truths beyond imagining. Denying igno­
rance, you pursue wondrous sights and endlessly 
unearth new facts. Undiscovered creatures, the cov­
etous dead, and jealous rivals impede your work, but 
in the pursuit of knowledge, no risk is too great.
