# Character Options

HEN YOU MAKE YOUR D&D CHARACTER, 
you have an array of options in the 
Player's Handbook to create the sort of 
adventurer you want. This chapter adds 
to those options, making it possible to realize even 
more character concepts. If you combine these op­
tions with those in Xanathar's Guide to Everything, 
the possibilities for your characters become vast. 
Here are the options featured in this chapter: 
• A way to customize your character's origin by 
changing some of your racial traits 
• Guidance on changing your subclass 
• The artificer class, a master of magical invention 
• Class features and subclasses for every class in 
the Player's Handbook 
• Feats for anyone who uses that optional rule
