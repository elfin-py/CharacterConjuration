# Otherworldly Glamour

3rd-level Fey Wanderer feature 
Your fey qualities give you a supernatural charm. 
As a result, whenever you make a Charisma check, 
you gain a bonus to the check equal to your Wisdom 
modifier (minimum of +l). 
In addition, you gain proficiency in one of the 
following skills of your choice: Deception, Perfor­
mance, or Persuasion.
