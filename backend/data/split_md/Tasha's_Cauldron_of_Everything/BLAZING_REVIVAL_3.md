# Blazing Revival

14th-level Circle of Wildfire feature 
The bond with your wildfire spirit can save you from 
death. If the spirit is within 120 feet of you when 
you are reduced to 0 hit points and thereby fall un­
conscious, you can cause the spirit to drop to 0 hit 
points. You then regain half your hit points and im­
mediately rise to your feet. 
· Once you use this feature, you can't use it again 
until you finish a long rest.
