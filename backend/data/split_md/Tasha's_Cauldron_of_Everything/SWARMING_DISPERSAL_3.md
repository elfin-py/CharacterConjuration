# Swarming Dispersal

15th-level Swarmkeeper feature 
You can discorporate into your swarm, avoiding 
danger. When you take damage, you can use your 
reaction to give yourself resistance to that damage. 
You vanish into your swarm and then teleport to an 
unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet of 
you, where you reappear with the swarm. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all ex­
pended uses when you finish a long rest.
