# Telekinetic Adept

7th-level Psi Warrior feature 
You have mastered new ways to use your telekinetic 
abilities, detailed below. 
Psi-Powered Leap. As a bonus action, you can 
propel your body with your mind. You gain a flying 
speed equal to twice your walking speed until the 
end of the current turn. Once you take this bonus 
action, you can't do so again until you finish a short 
or long rest, unless you expend a Psionic Energy die 
to take it again. 
Telekinetic Thrust. When you deal damage to a 
target with your Psionic Strike, you can force the 
target to make a Strength saving throw against a 
DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intel­
ligence modifier. If the save fails, you can knock the 
target prone or move it up to 10 feet in any direction 
horizontally.
