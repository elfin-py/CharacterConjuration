# Nature'S Mantle

Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by 
a druid or ranger) 
This cloak shifts color and texture to blend with the 
terrain surrounding you. While wearing the cloak, 
you can use it as a spellcasting focus for your druid 
and ranger spells. 
While you are in an area that is lightly obscured, 
you can Hide as a bonus action even if you are being 
directly observed.
