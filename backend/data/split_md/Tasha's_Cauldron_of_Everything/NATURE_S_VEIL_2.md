# Nature'S Veil

10th-level ranger feature, which replaces the Hide in 
Plain Sight feature 
You draw on the powers of nature to hide yourself 
from view briefly. As a bonus action, you can mag­
ically become invisible, along with any equipment 
you are wearing or carrying, until the start of your 
next turn. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all ex­
pended uses when you finish a long rest.
