# Halo Of Spores

2nd-level Circle of Spores feature 
You are surrounded by invisible, necrotic spores 
that are harmless until you unleash them on a crea­
ture nearby. When a creature you can see moves 
into a space within 10 feet of you or starts its turn 
there, you can use your reaction to deal ld4 necrotic 
damage to that creature unless it succeeds on a 
Constitution saving throw against your spell save 
DC. The necrotic damage increases to ld6 at 6th 
level, ld8 at 10th level, and ldlO at 14th level.
