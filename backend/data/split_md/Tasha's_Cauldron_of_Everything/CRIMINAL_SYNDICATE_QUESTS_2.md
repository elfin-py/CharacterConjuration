# Criminal Syndicate Quests

Your work as a syndicate member involves more 
than simple street swindles or pickpocketing. 
Someone with your skills cooperates with others 
for greater purposes that offer both dangerous 
risks and splendid rewards. The law of the land 
is your most persistent enemy, but other crimi­
nal syndicates challenge you as well-or become 
your targets. 
The Criminal Syndicate Quests table explores 
what kind of work you do for the organization.
