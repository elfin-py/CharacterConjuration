# D Readful Strikes

3rd-level Fey Wanderer feature 
You can augment your weapon strikes with 
mind-scarring magic, drawn from the gloomy hol­
lows of the Feywild. When you hit a creature with a 
weapon, you can deal an extra ld4 psychic damage 
to the target, which can take this extra damage only 
once per turn. 
The extra damage increases to ld6 when you 
reach 1 1th level in this class.
