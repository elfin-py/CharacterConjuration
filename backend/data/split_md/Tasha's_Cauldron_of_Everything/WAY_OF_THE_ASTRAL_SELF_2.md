A monk who follows the Way of the Astral Self be­
lieves their body is an illusion. They see their ki as a 
representation of their true form, an astral self. This 
astral self has the capacity to be a force of order or 
disorder, with some monasteries training students 
to use their power to protect the weak and other 
instructing aspirants in how to manifest their true 
selves in service to the mighty.
