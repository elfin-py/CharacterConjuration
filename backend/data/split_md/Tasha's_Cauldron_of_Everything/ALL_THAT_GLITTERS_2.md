# All That Glitters

Difficulty: Medium 
This gem-filled room can be placed in any dungeon, 
estate, or building with multiple rooms and might 
serve as both a tr<lp and a place to obtain a reward. 
Dozens of gems lie strewn upon the floor. Amid the 
treasure stands a marble statue with its hands clasped 
in front of it. A placard at the statue's base reads, 
"Only one treasure may leave this room. Cross with 
another and find your tomb."
