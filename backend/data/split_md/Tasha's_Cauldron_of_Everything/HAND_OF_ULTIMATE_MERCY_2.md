# Hand Of Ultimate Mercy

17th-level Way of Mercy feature 
Your mastery of life energy opens the door to the 
ultimate mercy. As an action, you can touch the 
corpse of a creature that died within the past 24 
hours and expend 5 ki points. The creature then re­
turns to life, regaining a number of hit points equal 
to 4d 10 + your Wisdom modifier. If the creature died 
while subject to any of the following conditions, it 
revives with them removed: blinded, deafened, para­
lyzed, poisoned, and stunned. 
Once you use this feature, you can't use it again 
until you finish a long rest.
