# Eye Of The Beholder

Difficulty: Medium 
This map puzzle is designed to lead a party through 
a dungeon where a roaming beholder doesn't wish 
to be disturbed. A series of clues tie to the word 
"eye," and the characters must determine how to get 
through the area safely. 
A disorienting wave sweeps over you. Suddenly, 
your surroundings are unfamiliar and shrouded 
i n  shadows. 
Out of the gloom appears a hooded goblin carrying 
a lantern. 
"Hello, friends!" the goblin says. "I can help you 
through these parts-if you can figure out my riddles. 
You don't want to make a wrong turn in here, as there 
are eyes everywhere. Solve the riddles and follow my 
directions to the letter." 
The goblin is friendly, and its offer is genuine. ft's 
name is Igor (pronounced eye-gar), which it reveals 
only if asked. The characters find themselves in 
a maze that emits magical darkness that can't be 
dispelled. No vision can penetrate this darkness, 
and only the goblin's lantern can illuminate it. Igor's 
lantern emits light in a 5-foot radius, but only so 
long as the goblin holds it. The lantern goes dark if 
any other creature takes custody of it.
