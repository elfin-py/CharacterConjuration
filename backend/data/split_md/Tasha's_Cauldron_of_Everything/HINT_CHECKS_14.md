# Hint Checks

Any character has the option of making these ability 
checks to receive a hint: 
Intelligence {Investigation) DC 10. The word 
"trace" from the Day 3 entry strikes the character 
as important, suggesting that the map is meant to 
be drawn on. 
Wisdom {Insight) DC 15. The map doesn't have 
labels, which means the names of the landmarks 
aren't significant. What's important are their posi­
tions relative to one another. 
Wisdom (Perception) DC 10. The fact that the ex­
pedition lasted three days is significant, as there 
are three dials. 
Wisdom (Survival) DC 15. The character knows 
the directions don't represent an efficient way to 
search an area and deduces the directions must 
be presenting some sort of message.
