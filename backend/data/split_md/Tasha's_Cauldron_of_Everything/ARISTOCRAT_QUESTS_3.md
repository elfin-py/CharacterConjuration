# Aristocrat Quests

A missive from your patron proffers a different kind 
of mission each time. For one assignment, you act 
as an envoy during delicate trade negotiations; the 
next, you're sent trekking through mountain passes 
to gather a favorite flower for a party. Foes are end­
less, and yesterday's ally might be tomorrow's tar­
get. The only things that are certain are the variety 
of your patron's whims and that tomorrow there will 
surely be more. 
The Aristocrat Quests table presents the sorts 
of work you might conduct at your highborn pa­
tron's request.
