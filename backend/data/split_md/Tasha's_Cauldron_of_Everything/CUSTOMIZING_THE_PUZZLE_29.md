# Customizing The Puzzle

Consider creating your own map and series of direc­
tions to customize this puzzle. By crafting directions 
that suit locations in your campaign's' setting, you 
can create a puzzle that's integrated into your adven­
ture's plot, using a map the characters might already 
possess. Your version of the puzzle can add as many 
digits and directions as you see fit, or it might reveal 
letters, symbols, or short words, depending on the 
complexity of your design.
