An alien influence has wrapped its tendrils around 
your mind, giving you psionic power. You can now 
touch other minds with that power and alter the 
world around you by using it to control the magical 
energy of the multiverse. Will this power shine from 
you as a hopeful beacon to others? Or will you be a 
source of terror to those who feel the stab of your 
mind and witness the strange manifestations of 
your might?
