The balm of peace thrives at the heart of healthy 
communities, between friendly nations, and in the 
souls of the kindhearted. The gods of peace inspire 
people of all sorts to resolve conflict and to stand up 
against those forces that try to prevent peace from 
flourishing. See the Peace Deities table for a list of 
some of the gods associated with this domain. 
Clerics of the Peace Domain preside over the sign­
ing of treaties, and they are often asked to arbitrate 
in disputes. These clerics' blessings draw people 
together and help them shoulder one another's 
burdens, and the clerics' magic aids those who are 
driven to fight for the way of peace.
