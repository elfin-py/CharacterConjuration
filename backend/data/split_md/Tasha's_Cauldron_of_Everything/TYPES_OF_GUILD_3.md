# Types Of Guild

The guild structure covers a swath of business ven­
tures, differentiated by their specialty. A conglom­
erate of blacksmiths, jewelers, carpenters, tailors, 
alchemists, scribes, and sages all could organize as 
a guild. Whatever their trade, these experts share 
contacts, exchange resources such as materials or 
tools, and leverage their collective influence to affect 
politics for their benefit. Alternatively, merchants 
and other business owners might also organize into 
guilds. Merchant barons who effectively rule a city 
or nation through iron-clad control of the economy 
or a network of innkeepers who share news and 
supply routes could both represent guild patrons. 
A guild could even embody a more sinister group, 
such as one that deals in terrifying wares like 
deadly monsters, dangerous knowledge, or souls. 
Roll or pick from the Guild Types table to deter­
mine the general sort of organization you oper­
ate within.
