# Sovereign Quests

The services you provide a sovereign largely depend 
on the nature of your group patron and their nation. 
While some of your missions involve official tasks­
missions undertaken in the sovereign's name-oth­
ers might be covert, making your patron's identity a 
highly guarded secret. Political rivals, enemy coun­
tries, and natural disasters all pose dangers to the 
sovereign's nation. Yet a sovereign who sows chaos, 
enacts tyrannical decrees, or jeopardizes a popu­
lation's way of life is likely to inspire rebellions. In 
such cases, a sovereign's agents must decide where 
their loyalties lie. 
The Sovereign Quests table presents a few of the 
sorts of missions you undertake for your liege.
