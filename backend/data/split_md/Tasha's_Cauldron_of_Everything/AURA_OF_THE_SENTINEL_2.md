# Aura Of The Sentinel

7th-level Oath of the Watchers feature 
You emit an aura of alertness while you aren't in­
capacitated. When you and any creatures of your 
choice within 10 feet of you roll initiative, you 
all gain a bonus to initiative equal to your profi­
ciency bonus. . 
At 18th level, the range of this aura increases 
to 30 feet.
