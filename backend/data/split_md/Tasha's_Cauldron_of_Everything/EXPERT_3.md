# Expert

The Expert is a master of certain tasks or knowl­
edge, favoring cunning over brawn. It might be a 
scout, a musician, a librarian, a clever street kid, a 
wily merchant, or a burglar. 
To gain the Expert class, a creature must have at 
least one language in its stat block that it can speak. 
A sidekick gains the following class features as it 
gains levels, as summarized on the Expert table.
