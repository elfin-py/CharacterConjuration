# Psychic Veil

13th-level Soulknife feature 
You can weave a veil of psychic static to mask 
yourself. As an action, you can magically become 
invisible, along with anything you are wearing or 
carrying, for 1 hour or until you dismiss this effect 
(no action required). This invisibility ends early im­
mediately after you deal damage to a creature or you 
force a creature to make a saving throw. 
Once you use this feature, you can't do so again 
until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a 
Psionic Energy die to use this feature again.
