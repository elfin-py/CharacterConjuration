# Restore Balance

1st-level Clockwork Soul feature 
Your connection to the plane of absolute order al­
lows you to equalize chaotic moments. When a crea­
ture you can see within 60 feet of you is about to roll 
a d20 with advantage or disadvantage, you can use 
your reaction to prevent the roll from being affected 
by advantage and disadvantage. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all ex­
pended uses when you finish a long rest.
