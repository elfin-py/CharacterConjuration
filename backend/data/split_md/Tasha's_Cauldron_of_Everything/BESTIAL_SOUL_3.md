# Bestial Soul

6th-level Path of the Beast feature 
The feral power within you increases, causing the 
natural weapons of your Form of the Beast to count 
as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance 
and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage. 
You can also alter your form to help you adapt to 
your surroundings. When you finish a short or long 
rest, choose one of the following benefits, which 
lasts until you finish your next short or long rest: 
• You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking 
speed, and you can breathe underwater. 
• You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking 
speed, and you can climb difficult surfaces, includ­
ing upside down on ceilings, without needing to 
make an ability check. 
• When you jump, you can make a Strength (Athlet­
ics) check and extend your jump by a number of 
feet equal to the check's total. You can make this 
special check only once per turn.
