Magic of the book-that's what many folk call wiz­
ardry. The name is apt, given how much time wiz­
ards spend poring over tomes and penning theories 
about the nature of magic. It's rare to see wizards 
traveling without books and scrolls sprouting from 
their bags, and a wizard would go to great lengths to 
plumb an archive of ancient knowledge. 
Among wizards, the Order of Scribes is the most 
bookish. It takes many forms in different worlds, 
but its primary mission is the same everywhere: 
recording magical discoveries so that wizardry can 
flourish. And while all wizards value spellbooks, a 
wizard in the Order of Scribes magically awakens 
their book, turning it into a trusted companion. All 
wizards study books, but a wizardly scribe talks 
to theirs!
