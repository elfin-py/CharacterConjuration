# Physician'S Touch

6th-leve.l Way of Mercy feature 
You can administer even greater cures with a touch, 
and if you feel it's necessary, you can use your 
knowledge to cause harm. 
When you use Hand of Healing on a creature, you 
can also end one disease or one of the following 
conditions affecting the creature: blinded, deafened, 
paralyzed, poisoned, or stunned. 
When you use Hand of Harm on a creature, you 
can subject that creature to the poisoned condition 
until the end of your next turn.
