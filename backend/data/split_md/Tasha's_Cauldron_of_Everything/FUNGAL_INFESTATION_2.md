# Fungal Infestation

6th-level Circle of Spores feature 
Your spores gain the ability to infest a corpse and 
animate it. If a beast or a humanoid that is Small 
or Medium dies within 10 feet of you, you can use 
your reaction to animate it, causing it to stand up 
immediately with 1 hit point. The creature uses 
the Zombie stat block in the Monster Manual. It 
remains animate for 1 hour, after which time it col­
lapses and dies. 
In combat, the zombie's turn comes immediately 
after yours. It obeys your mental commands, and 
the only action it can take is the Attack action, mak­
ing one melee attack. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal 
to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and 
you regain all expended uses of it when you finish a 
long rest.
