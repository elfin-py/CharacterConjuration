# Aura Of Alacrity

7th-level Oath of Glory feature 
You emanate an aura that fills you and your com­
panions with supernatural speed, allowing you to 
race across a battlefield in formation. Your walking 
speed increases by 10 feet. In addition, if you aren't 
incapacitated, the walking speed of any ally who 
starts their turn within 5 feet of you increases by 10 
feet until the end of that turn. 
When you reach 18th level in this class, the range 
of the aura increases to 10 feet.
