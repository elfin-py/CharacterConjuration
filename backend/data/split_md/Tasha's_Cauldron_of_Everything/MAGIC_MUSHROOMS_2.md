# Magic Mushrooms

Mushrooms can be deadly, delicious, or both. Some 
have magical properties, especially those that grow 
in areas suffused by mystical energy, such as the 
Underdark and the Feywild. 
Creatures proficient in the Medicine, Nature, or 
Survival skills might be versed on the subject of 
fungi, especially the magical kind, since the benefi­
cial effects can save lives or bestow unusual powers. 
But when an unknown variety of fungus is encoun­
tered, only an expert can identify it and determine 
its properties. 
To determine the effects of eating such fungus, 
roll on the Magic Mushroom Effects table.
