# Additional Wizard Spells

1st-level wizard feature 
The spells in the following list expand the wizard 
spell list in the Player's Handbook. The list is or­
ganized by spell level, not character level. A spell's 
school of magic is noted, and if a spell can be cast as 
a ritual, the ritual tag appears after the spell's name. 
Each spell is in the Player's Handbook, unless it has 
an asterisk (a spell in chapter 3). Xanathar's Guide 
to Everything also offers more spells.
