# Vigilant Rebuke

15th-level Oath of the Watchers feature 
You've learned how to chastise anyone who dares 
wield beguilements against you and your wards. 
Whenever you or a creature you can see within 30 
feet of you succeeds on an Intelligence, a Wisdom, 
or a Charisma saving throw, you can use your re­
action to deal 2d8 + your Charisma modifier force 
damage to the creature that forced the saving throw.
