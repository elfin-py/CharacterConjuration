Spell Slots. The Spellcaster table shows how 
many spell slots the sidekick has to cast its Spell­
caster spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of 
these spells, the sidekick must expend a slot of the 
spell's level or higher. The sidekick regains all ex­
pended spell slots when it finishes a long rest. 
. Spells Known. The sidekick knows two cantrips 
and one 1st-level spell of your choice from its spell 
list. Here are recommendations for a 1st-level spell­
caster of each role: 
Mage: mage hand, ray of frost, thunderwave 
Healer: cure wounds, guidance, sacred flame 
Prodigy: eldritch blast, healing word, light
