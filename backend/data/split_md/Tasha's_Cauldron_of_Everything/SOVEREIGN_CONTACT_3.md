# Sovereign Contact

You might benefit from direct contact with your 
group patron. This includes audiences or secret 
meetings with the sovereign, depending on the na­
ture of your work. Alternatively, the sovereign might 
purposefully wantto keep their distance from you, 
either due to their busy schedules or to maintain 
plausible deniability regarding your work. In such 
cases, an advisor or functionary oversees your as­
signments, serving as the primary contact between 
you and the sovereign. 
Roll or pick from the Sovereign Contact table to 
determine who manages the relationship between 
you and the throne, if not the sovereign directly.
