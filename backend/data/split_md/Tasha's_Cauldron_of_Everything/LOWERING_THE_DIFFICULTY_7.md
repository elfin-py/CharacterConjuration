# Lowering The Difficulty

To make the puzzle easier to solve, a spirit can pro­
vide a hint in addition to giving its name. Coaxing a 
hint from a spirit requires a successful DC 1 5  Cha­
risma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check. Consider 
hints like "A is the first letter in the alphabet" and 
"The gemstones, in order, will help for a spell."
