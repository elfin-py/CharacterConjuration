# Unarmed Fighting

Your unarmed strikes can deal bludgeoning damage 
equal to ld6 + your Strength modifier on a hit. If you 
aren't wielding any weapons or a shield when you 
make the attack roll, the d6 becomes a d8. 
At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 
ld4 bludgeoning damage to one creature grap­
pled by you.
