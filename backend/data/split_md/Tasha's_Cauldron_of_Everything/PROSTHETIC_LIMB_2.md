# Prosthetic Limb

Wondrous item, common 
This item replaces a lost limb-a hand, an arm, a 
foot, a leg, or a similar body part. While the pros­
thetic is attached, it functions identically to the 
part it replaces. You can detach or reattach it as an 
action, and it can't be removed against your will. It 
detaches if you die.
