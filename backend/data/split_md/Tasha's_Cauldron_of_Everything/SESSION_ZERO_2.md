Before making characters or playing the game, the 
DM and players can run a special session-colloqui­
ally called session zero-to establish expectations, 
outline the terms of a social contract, and share 
house rules. Making and sticking to these rules can 
help ensure that the game is a fun experience for 
everyone involved. 
Often a session zero includes building characters 
together. As the DM, you can help players during 
the character creation process by advising them to 
select options that will serve the adventure or cam­
paign that awaits.
