# Tasha

The Order Domain represents discipline, as well as 
devotion to the laws that govern a society, an insti­
tution, or a philosophy. Clerics of Order meditate on 
logic and justice as they serve their gods, examples 
of which appear in the Order Deities table. 
Clerics of Order believe that well-crafted laws es­
tablish legitimate hierarchies, and those selected by 
law to lead must be obeyed. Those who obey must 
do so to the best of their ability, and if those who 
lead fail to protect the law, they must be replaced. In 
this manner, law weaves a web of obligations that 
create order and security in a chaotic multiverse.
