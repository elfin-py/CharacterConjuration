# H Int Checks

Any character has the option of making these ability 
checks to receive a hint: 
Intelligence (Arcana) DC 10. The character recalls 
up to three of the material components for spells 
on the list they don't currently know. 
Wisdom (Insight) DC 15. The character suspects 
that the jumbled letters in the middle-or some 
number of them-probably spell out a command 
word, pass-phrase, or important clue. 
Wisdom (Perception) DC 10. The character notices 
that straight lines drawn between spells and mate­
rial components cross through some of the letters 
in the center.
