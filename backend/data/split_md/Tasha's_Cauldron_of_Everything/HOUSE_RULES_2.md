# House Rules

House rules include optional rules, such as those 
presented in chapter 9 of the Dungeon Master's 
Guide, and rules you create. If you plan to use any 
house rules, session zero is a good time to discuss 
those rules with the players and solicit their input. 
House rules are best presented as experiments, 
and time will tell if they're good for your game. If 
you introduce a house rule in session zero that 
turns out to have an adverse effect on people's en­
joyment of the game, you may jettison or revise the 
house rule to create a better gaming experience for 
everyone. Don't feel bad if a house rule doesn't end 
up working as well as you expected it to. Remember: 
the goal is to ensure everyone is having fun.
