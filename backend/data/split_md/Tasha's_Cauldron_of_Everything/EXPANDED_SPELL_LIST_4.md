# Expanded Spell List

1st-level Genie feature 
The Genie lets you choose from an expanded list of 
spells when you learn a warlock spell. The Genie 
Expanded Spells table shows the genie spells that 
are added to the warlock spell list for you, along 
with the spells associated in the table with your pa­
tron's kind: dao, djinni, efreeti, or marid.
