# Outrider

Fighting Style: Archery 
Maneuvers: Ambush,* Distracting Strike, Goading 
Attack, Precision Attack, Quick Toss* 
Feats: Alert, Crossbow Expert, Mounted Combat-
ant, Observant, Sharpshooter 
You find freedom in the saddle and a companion 
in your mount. A headlong charge into combat is a 
blunt instrument for oafs. You prefer mobility and 
range, opting to find advantageous positions that al­
low you to deal with foes at full gallop while evading 
the most dangerous threats.
