# Religious Order Member

Your primary duty to a religious order is to further 
your god's reach. That obligation ranges from pros­
elytizing or performing religious services to meting 
out divine punishments or recovering lost relics. Be­
yond that, the needs of your order vary widely. Your 
patron relies on your group due to your particular 
skills or, perhaps, because it's divine whim. 
The Religious Order Member Roles table suggests 
positions you might fill in an order and the back­
grounds frequently associated with each role.
