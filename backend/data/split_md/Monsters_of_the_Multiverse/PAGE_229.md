STAR SPAWN LARVA MAGE 
A larva mage is a nightmarish combination of a 
mortal body and otherworldly substance. When a 
powerful cultist of a wormlike entity such as Kyuss 
or Kezef-usually a warlock or other spellcaster -
contacts the comet-borne emissary of an Elder Evil, 
the emissary can merge with a mortal conscious-
ness to create a larva mage. None of the original 
cultist's personality survives the transformation; 
what emerges is wholly alien.
