LEVIATHAN 
A leviathan is an immense creature that acts as a 
force of nature , dragging ships down to the ocean's 
depths and washing away coastal settlements. 
When called forth, a leviathan arises from a large 
body of water and takes on the form of a gigan-
tic serpent. 
Usually found only on the Elemental Plane of 
Water , a leviathan sometimes swims through a por-
tal to another world, where tritons, sea elves, and 
other aquatic folk attempt to contain it. Nihilistic 
cults have also been known to perform arduous 
rituals to summon a leviathan to a world, with the 
aim of using the creature to destroy coastal commu-
nities. Those cultists often consider it a blessing to • 
drown themselves in the elemental's waters.
