HELLFIRE ENGINE 
Hellfire engines are semiautonomous bringers 
of destruction. Amnizus (in this book) and other 
devilish generals hold them in reserve until they 
are needed to repel an incursion by demons or 
crusading mortals, but occasionally one of these 
magical-mechanical hybrids gets loose , driven ber-
serk by its need to destroy. 
Hellfire engines take many forms, but all of them 
have one purpose: to mow down foes in waves. They 
are incapable of subtlety or trickery, but their de-
structive capability is immense. 
Mortal creatures slain by hellfire engines are 
doomed to join the infernal legions in mere hours 
unless powerful magic-wielders intervene on their 
behalf. The archdukes of the Nine Hells would like 
nothing better than to modify this magic so it works 
against demons, too, but that discovery has eluded 
them so far.
