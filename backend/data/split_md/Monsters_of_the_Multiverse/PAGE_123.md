_Source: Monsters of the Multiverse.pdf, page 123_

ELEMENTAL MYRMIDONS 
Elemental myrmidons are Elementals conjured and 
bound by magic into ritually created suits of plate 
armor . In this form , they possess no recollection of 
their former existence as free Elementals. They ex-
ist only to follow the commands of their creators.
