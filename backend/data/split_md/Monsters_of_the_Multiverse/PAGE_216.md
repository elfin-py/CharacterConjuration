# SHADOW MASTIFFS

_Source: Monsters of the Multiverse.pdf, page 216_

SHADOW MASTIFFS 
Shadow mastiffs -hounds of the Shadowfell-move 
invisibly through the shadows, always on the hunt. 
SHADOW MASTIFF 
Shunning sunlight, these hounds are usually met as 
a pack. Some faiths devoted to deities of gloom and 
night, such as that of Shar in the Forgotten Realms, 
perform unholy rites to summon shadow mastiffs to 
work as temple sentinels and bodyguards. 
SHADOW MASTIFF ALPHA 
Each pack of shadow mastiffs is led by an alpha , the 
smartest one of the group. The sound of an alpha's 
howl strikes terror into those who hear it and is a 
sure sign that a pack is on the prowl.
