DINOSAURS 
The Monster Manual has statistics for several kinds 
of dinosaurs. This section provides several more. 
BRONTOSAURUS 
This massive four-legged dinosaur is large enough 
that most predators leave it alone. Its deadly tail can 
drive away or kill smaller threats. 
DEINONYCHUS 
This larger cousin of the velociraptor kills by grip-
ping its target with its claws and feeding. 
DIMETRODON 
This sail-backed reptile is commonly found in areas 
where dinosaurs live. It hunts on shores and in shal-
low water, filling a similar role to a crocodile.
