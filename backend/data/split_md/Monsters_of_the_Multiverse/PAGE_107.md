DuERGAR 
Duergar are dwarves of the deep reaches of the Un-
derdark and other sunless realms. Their personali-
ties and abilities have been deeply impacted by their 
ancestors ' captivity and torment by mind flayers; 
they were infused with powerful psionic abilities but 
also a profound gloom. In some , this strain of sor-
row inspires works of grand but melancholic beauty, 
while in others, it manifests as rage. 
Like many who dwell in the Underdark, duergar 
must constantly be on guard against the raids and 
plots of their neighbors. To this end, duergar war-
riors fulfill a variety of combat roles, often marrying 
their fury in battle with their psionic abilities or 
training dang erous Underdark creatures as mounts .
