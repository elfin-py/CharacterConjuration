248 CHAPTER 2 I BESTIARY VENOM TROLL 
A troll that survives massive doses of poison might 
transform into a venom troll. Lingering poison in-
fuses the troll's blood and tissue, and poison leaks 
from the pores to coat the troll's fangs and claws. 
These creatures are especially dangerous in close 
combat because poison drips off their flesh and 
sprays out from every wound they receive.
