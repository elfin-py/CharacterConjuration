WARLORD 
Warlords are legendary battlefield commanders, 
whose names are spoken with awe. After a string 
of decisive victories, a warlord could easily take on 
the role of monarch or general and attract followers 
willing to die for the warlord 's banner. 
Warlords urge their troops into the fray with 
shouted exhortations. You can roll on the Warlord 
Battle Cries table to select one, or choose a battle 
cry that fits with your campaign .
