0RTHON 
When an archduke of the Nine Hells needs a crea-
ture tracked, found, and either done away with or 
captured, the task usually falls to an orthon. These 
devils are infernal bounty hunters, tireless in their 
pursuit of their quarry across the multi verse. 
Orthons are infamous for their sharp senses. 
Because an orthon can become invisible at will, its 
quarry is often unaware of being hunted until the or-
thon strikes. This invisibility can be disrupted when 
the devil is attacked, however, so a strong counterat-
tack is often the best defense against it. 
Orthons value the challenge of the chase and the 
thrill of one-on-one combat above all else. An or-
thon's first loyalty is to its archduke, but one with no 
immediate assignment might work for anyone who 
promises it a worthy struggle against a lethal foe. 
Because they travel widely, orthons are unequaled 
as guides through the layers of the Nine Hells.
