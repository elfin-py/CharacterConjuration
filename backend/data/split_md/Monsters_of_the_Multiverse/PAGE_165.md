_Source: Monsters of the Multiverse.pdf, page 165_

KOBOLD INVENTOR 
A kobold inventor builds improvised weapons to 
gain an advantage in combat. These weapons last 
for only one or two attacks before they break and 
typically work only for the inventor, but they might 
be surprisingly effective in the meantime. The weap-
ons don't have to be lethal -often one serves its pur-
pose if it distracts, scares, or confuses a creature 
long enough for the inventor to kill that foe.
