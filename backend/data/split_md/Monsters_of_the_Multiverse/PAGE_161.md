CULTISTS OF JUIBLEX 
The Faceless Lord grants its cultists special abili-
ties. Lesser followers can gain the Liquid Movement 
trait. The most dedicated devotees of ooze can also 
gain the Slimy Organs trait. 
Liquid Movement. As an action, this creature can move up to 
20 feet through spaces no more than an inch in diameter. It 
must end this movement in a space that can accommodate its 
full size. Otherwise, it takes 5 force damage and returns to the 
space where it began this movement . 
Slimy Organs. This creature has resistance to bludgeoning , 
piercing , and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks. 
Whenever this creature suffers a critical hit or is reduced to 0 
hit points, it sprays acid; each creature within 5 feet of it takes 
acid damage equal to its number of Hit Dice.
