# Elixir Of Health

Potion, rare 
Summoned Elemental 
Air elemental 
Earth elemental 
Fire elemental 
Water elemental 
When you drink this potion, it cures any disease 
afflicting you, and it removes the blinded, deafened, 
paralyzed, and poisoned conditions. The clear red liquid 
has tiny bubbles of light in it.
