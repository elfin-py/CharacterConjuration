# Sun Blade

Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement) 
This item appears to be a longsword hilt. While 
grasping the hilt, you can use a bonus action to cause a 
blade of pure radiance to spring into existence, or make 
the blade disappear. While the blade exists, this magic 
longsword has the finesse property. If you are proficient 
with shortswords or longswords, you are proficient with 
the sun blade.
