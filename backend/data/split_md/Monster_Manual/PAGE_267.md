SALAMANDERS Heated Weapons. Any metal melee weapon the salamander

ACTIONS 
Multiattack. The salamander makes two attacks: one with its 
spear and one with its tail. 
Spear. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. 
or range 20 ft./60ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing 
damage, or 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage if used with two 
hands to make a melee attack, plus 3 (1d6) fire damage. 
Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 
Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) fire 
damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 14). Until this 
grapple ends, the target is restrained, the salamander can 
automatica lly hit the target with its tail, and the salamander 
can't make tail attacks against other targets.
