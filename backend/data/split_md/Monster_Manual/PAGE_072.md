# Some bone devils have the following action options.

_Source: Monster Manual.pdf, page 72_

Some bone devils have the following action options. 
Multiattack. The devil makes two attacks: one with its 
hooked polearm and one with its sting. 
Hooked Polearm. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 
10ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d12 + 4) piercing damage. If the 
target is a Huge or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape 
DC 14). Until this grapple ends, the devil can't use its 
polearm on another target.

ACTIONS 
Multiattack. The devil makes three attacks: two with its claws 
and one with its sting. 
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10ft., one target. 
Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage. 
Sting. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10ft., one target. 
Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) poison 
damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution 
saving throw or become poisoned for 1 minute. The target can 
repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending 
the effect on itself on a success.
