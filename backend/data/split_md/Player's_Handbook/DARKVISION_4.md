# Darkvision

2nd-levei transmutation
Casting Time: I action
Range: Touch
Components:
V.S, M (either a pinch of dried
carrot or an agate)
Duration:
8 hours
Vou touch a willing creature
to grant it the ability to
see in the dark. For the duration. that creature
has
darkvision out to a range of 60 feel.
