# School Of Divination

The counsel of a diviner is sought by royalty and
commoners
alike, for ali seek a clearer understanding
of the past, present, and future. As a diviner, you strive
to part the veils of space, time, and consciousness
so
that you can see clearly. Vou work to master spells of
discernment,
remote viewing, supernatural
knowledge,
and foresight.
