# Powers

Sorcerers
are rare in the world, and it's unusual to find
a sorcerer who is not involved in the adventuring
life
in some way. People with magical power seething in
their veins soon discover that the power doesn't like to
stay quiet. A sorcerer's
magic wants to be wielded. and
it has a tendency to spill out in unpredictable
ways if
it isn't called on.
