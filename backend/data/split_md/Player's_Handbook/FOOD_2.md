# Food

A character
needs one pound of food per day and can
make food last longer by subsisting
on half rations.
Eating half a pound of food in a day counts as half a day
without food.
A character
can go without food for a number of days
equal to 3 + his or her Constitution
modifier (minimum
I). At the end of each day beyond that limit, a character
automatically suffers one levei of exhaustion.
A normal day of eating resets the count of days
without food to zero.
