# Suggestlon

2nd-levei enchantment
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components:
V,M (a snake's tongue and either a bit of
honeycomb or a drop of sweet oil)
Duration:
Concentration,
up to 8 hours
Vou suggest a course of activity (limited to a sentence
or two) and magically influence a creature you can
see within range that can hear and understand
YOU.
Creatures
that can't be charmed are immune to this
effec!. The suggestion
must be worded in such a manner
as to make the course of action sound reasonable.
Asking the creature
to stab itself, throw itself onto
a spear, immolate itself, or do some other obviously
harmful act ends the spell.
The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a
failed save, it pursues the course of action you described
to the best of its ability. The suggested course of action
can continue for the entire duration. Ir the suggested
activity can be completed in a shorter time, the spell
ends when the subject tinishes what it was asked to do.
Vou can also specify conditions lhat will trigger a
special activity during the duration. For example, you
might suggest that a knight give her warhorse
to the
tirst beggar she meets. Ir the condition isn't met before
the spell expires, the activity isn't performed.
Ir you or any of your companions
damage the target,
the spell ends.
