# Expenses

Between adventures,
you choose a particular
quality
of !ife and pay the cost of maintaining
that lifestyle, as
described
in chapter 5.
Living a particular
lifestyle doesn't have a huge effect
on your character, but your lifestyle can affect the way
other individuais and groups react to you. For example,
when you lead an aristocratic
lifestyle, it might be easier
for you to influence the nobles of the city than if you
live in poverty.
