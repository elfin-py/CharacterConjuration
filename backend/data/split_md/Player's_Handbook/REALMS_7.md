# Realms

Dozens of deities are revered, worshiped,
and feared
throughout
the world of the Forgotten Realms. At least
thirly deities are widely known across the Realms, and
many more are worshiped
locally, by individual tribes,
small cults, or certain sects of larger religious tem pies.
Each world in the D&D multiverse has its own
pantheons
of deities, ranging in size from the teeming
pantheons
of the Forgotten Realms and Greyhawk to
the more focused religions of Eberron and Dragonlance.
Many of the nonhuman
races worship the same gods on
different worlds-Moradin,
for example, is revered by
dwarves of the Forgotten Realms, Greyhawk, and many
other worlds.
