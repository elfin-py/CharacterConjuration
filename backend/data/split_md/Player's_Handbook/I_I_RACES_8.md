Whenever you make an Intelligence
(History) check related to the origin of stonework, you
are considered
proficient in the History skill and add
double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of
your normal proficiency bonus.
Languages. Vou can speak, read, and write Common
and Owarvish. Owarvish is full of hard consonants
and
guttural sounds, and those characteristics
spill over into
whatever other language a dwarf might speak.

worlds of 0&0: hill dwarves and mountain dwarves.
