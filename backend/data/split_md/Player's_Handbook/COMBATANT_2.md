Vou are a dangerous
foe to face while mounted. While
you are mounted and aren't incapacitated,
you gain the
following benefits:
Vou have advantage on melee attack rolls against any
unmounted
creature
that is smaller than your mounl.
Vou can force an attack targeted at your mount to tar-
get you instead.
Ir your mount is subjected to an effect that allows it to
