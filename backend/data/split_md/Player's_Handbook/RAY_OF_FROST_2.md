# Ray Of Frost

Evocation cantrip
Casting Time: I action
Range: 60 feet
Components:
V, 5
Duration: Instantaneous
A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a
creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack
against the target. On a hit, it takes ld8 cold damage,
and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of
your next turno
The spell's damage increases
by Id8 when you reach
5th levei (2d8), 11th levei (3d8), and 17th levei (4d8).
