# Of Vitality

3rd-level evocation
Casting Time: I action
Range: Self (30-foot radius)
Components:
V
Duration:
Concentration,
up to I minute
Healing energy radiates from you in an aura with a
30-foot radius. Until the spell ends, the aura moves with
you, centered on you. Vou can use a bonus action to
cause one creature
in the aura (including you) to regain
2d6 hit points.
