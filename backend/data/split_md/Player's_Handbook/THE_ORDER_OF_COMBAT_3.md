# The Order Of Combat

A typical combat encounter is a clash between two
sides, a f1urry of weapon swings, feints, parries,
footwork, and spellcasting.
The game organizes the
chaos of combat into a cycle of rounds and turns. A
round
represents
about 6 seconds in the game world.
During a round, each participant
in a battle takes a
turno The order of turns is determined
at the beginning
of a combat encounter, when everyone rolls initiative.
Once everyone has taken a turn, the fight continues
to
the next round if neither side has defeated the other.
