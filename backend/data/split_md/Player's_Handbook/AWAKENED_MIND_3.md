# Awakened Mind

Starting
at 1st levei, your alien knowledge gives you
the ability to touch the minds of other creatures.
Vou
can communicate
telepathically with any creature you
can see within 30 feet ofyou. Vou don't need to share
a language with the creature
for it to understand
your
telepathic utterances,
but the creature
must be able to
understand
at least one language.
