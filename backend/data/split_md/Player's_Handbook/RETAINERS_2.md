# Retainers

If your character
has a noble background,
you may select this
background
feature
instead
of Position
of Privilege.
You have the service ofthree
retainers
loyal to your family.
These retainers
can be attendants
or messengers,
and one
might be a majordomo.
Your retainers
are commoners
who
can perform
mundane
tasks for you, but they do not fight
for you, will not follow you into obviously
dangerous
areas
(such as dungeons).
and willleave
ifthey are frequently
endangered
or abused.
