# Gnomes

A third
subrace
of gnomes,
the deep
gnomes
(or svirfneblin),
live in small
communities
scattered
in the Underdark.
Unlike
the duergar
and the drow, svirfneblin
are as good
as their
surface cousins. However, their humor and enthusiasm
are dampened
by their oppressive
environment,
and their
inventive
expertise
is directed
mostly
toward
stonework.
