# Part

3 I SPELLS
Place a suggestion in one location. Vou select an area
of up to 5 feet square, and any creature
that enters
or passes through the area receives the suggestion
mentally.
The whole warded area radiates magic. A dispel
magic cast on a specific effect, if successful,
removes
only that effect.
Vou can create a permanently
guarded and warded
structure
by casting this spell there every day
for one year.
