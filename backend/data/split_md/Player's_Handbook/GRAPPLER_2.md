# Grappler

Prerequisite: Strength
13 ar higher
You've developed the skills necessary
to hold your
own in close-quarters
grappling. Vou gain the
following benefits:
Vou have advantage on attack rolls against a creature
you are grappling.
Vou can use your action to try to pin a creature grap-
pled by you. To do so, make another grapple check. lf
you succeed, you and the creature are both restrained
until the grapple ends.
Creatures
that are one size larger than you don't auto-
matically succeed on checks to escape your grapple.
