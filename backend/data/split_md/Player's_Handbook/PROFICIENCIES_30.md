# Proficiencies

Each background
gives a character
proticiency in two
skills. SkilIs are described
in chapter 7.
In addition, most backgrounds
give a character
proticiency with one or more tools. Tools and tool
proticiencies are detailed in chapter 5.
If a character
would gain the same proticiency from
two different sources, he or she can choose a different
proticiency of the same kind (skilI or tool) instead.
