# Stoneskin

4th-levei abjuration
Casting Time: I action
Range: Touch
Components:
V,S, M (diamond dust worth 100 gp,
which the spell consumes)
Duration:
COllcentration, up to I hour


This spell turns the flesh of a willing creature you touch
as hard as stone. Until the spell ends, the target has
resistance
to nonmagical
bludgeoning, piercing, and
slashing damage.
